Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers

How secure is my data?

We use industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your data. Your information is stored in secure, encrypted databases with regular backups and 24/7 monitoring.

Can I export my data?

Yes! TaskFlow supports data export in multiple formats including CSV, JSON, and Excel. You can export your data anytime with just a few clicks.

What's included in the free plan?

Our free plan includes up to 100 tasks, basic views (list and board), personal calendar integration, and mobile access. Perfect for individual users!

How does the AI assistance work?

Our AI analyzes your work patterns and task history to provide smart suggestions for task prioritization, scheduling, and team allocation. It learns and improves over time!
